Biggest news

Shaman: Rage of the Far Seer loses casting speed

Totes Alot (Tauren Shaman): the faster casting was part of what made Enhancement a smooth leveler. Your instant Maelstrom Lightning Bolts are unaffected and still carry the spec, but the stronger Lightning Bolt helps Elemental. Compare the two before you commit at level 30.
